01 — Journey

From first click to repeat deposit

The full path a player takes, with the team accountable for each stage and the decision embedded in it.

01

Discovery Affiliates · SEO · Social

Player lands on the casino site via affiliate link, organic search, or social. Acquisition cost and channel quality are set here — everything downstream inherits this traffic's intent.

→ Action: Tag every session by source at landing, not at deposit — you can't fix a channel you can't attribute.
02

Registration Product · UX

Browses games and offers, registers, verifies email or phone. This is the first hard drop-off point — friction here is pure loss, no second chance.

→ Action: Cut required fields to the legal minimum at signup; move KYC-adjacent data collection to first-withdrawal, not registration.
03

First Deposit Payments

The account becomes a First Time Depositor (FTD). The welcome bonus decision happens right here.

ClaimsWelcome bonus — enters a wagering-requirement path, higher short-term engagement.
SkipsNo bonus — cleaner balance, often a higher-value or more cautious player.
→ Action: Segment CRM messaging by this choice on day one — bonus-skippers respond to cashback, not free spins.
04

Gameplay Gaming Operations

Player picks a product and plays. Product choice at this stage predicts long-term value more than any acquisition variable.

Slots
Live Casino
Sportsbook
Cross-product play (highest LTV cohort)
→ Action: Trigger a cross-sell nudge (e.g. live-casino free bet) the first time a slots-only player hits a session-length milestone.
05

Outcome CRM · Promotions

Every session resolves to a win or a loss, and each triggers a different CRM path.

LoseBalance hits zero → refill decision → ~65% re-deposit* and continue playing.
WinRequests a withdrawal → moves to KYC.
→ Action: Fire a time-boxed reload offer within minutes of a zero-balance event — the 65% re-deposit window is short.
06

KYC Verification Compliance · Support

Withdrawal triggers identity verification, usually mandatory. This is the highest-trust, highest-abandonment moment in the whole lifecycle.

Self-service document upload
Assisted — customer support handles document issues
→ Action: Pre-collect ID documents at deposit #2, not at first withdrawal — decouple compliance friction from payout anxiety.
07

Withdrawal Payments

KYC approved, funds released. A fast, transparent payout is the single biggest driver of long-term trust and reactivation willingness.

→ Action: Publish an explicit payout-time SLA in-product; silence during processing is what drives support tickets and churn.
08

Retention Loop CRM · VIP Team

Cashback, reload bonuses, free spins, sports boosts and VIP perks pull the player back to deposit — and the journey restarts at gameplay, not at discovery.

→ Action: Route every returning player straight to step 04 (skip re-onboarding) — treat retention traffic as a different funnel entirely from acquisition traffic.

04 — The Loop

Retention closes the circle

Six CRM levers pull a withdrawn or lapsed player back into gameplay — restarting the cycle without going back through acquisition.

VIP tiering

Escalating perks for high-value players — protects your highest-LTV cohort from competitor poaching.

Cashback

Partial loss-rebate, scheduled and predictable — softens the sting of a losing session.

Reload bonuses

Deposit-matched offers timed to lapsing players — the direct trigger back into step 03/04.

Free spins & sports boosts

Low-cost, high-frequency nudges that keep the app top-of-mind between sessions.

CRM orchestration

Decides who gets which lever, when — the actual engine driving the loop, not a single campaign.

05 — Playbook

What to do with this map

Three places to focus first, with the KPI that tells you if it's working.

Fix the leak

Registration → First Deposit

  • Reduce signup fields to email/phone only
  • Offer 2–3 deposit methods max, ranked by local success rate
  • A/B test bonus-vs-no-bonus framing at the deposit screen
KPI → Reg-to-FTD conversion rate
Widen the loop

Loss → Re-deposit window

  • Trigger reload offer within 15 minutes of zero balance
  • Cap offer frequency to avoid fatigue on high-frequency losers
  • Route by product — sportsbook vs. slots losers need different hooks
KPI → Re-deposit rate within 24h of loss
Remove the friction

Withdrawal → KYC

  • Pre-collect ID at second deposit, not first withdrawal
  • Publish payout SLA inside the withdrawal screen itself
  • Route flagged documents to support before the player asks
KPI → Time from withdrawal request to funds received
